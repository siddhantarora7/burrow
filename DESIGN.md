# Design System: burrow

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Lit Garden"**

burrow is a cozy 16-bit zen garden drawn into one low-resolution canvas and scaled up by a whole number: Mt Fuji behind a forest band, a tiled wall with white plaster lines, a momiji maple, raked gravel, a koi pond, a stone lantern, bushes and moss. The sky is the visitor's own clock. Every scene colour has a day, dusk and night variant, and the garden relights itself by interpolating between them, so the same page reads as noon, sunset or a starry night without a second asset.

The pets are the only things allowed to shout. They are 32 px sprites with a 1 px dark-plum outline, rim light from the upper left, big glinting eyes, blush and tiny feet, and they keep their own fixed tones while the scene darkens around them. Their state is told by pose and props (a laptop, a speech bubble, crumbs, sparkles, a z), never by colour alone, and is restated in a frosted name tag below each stone.

Over the pixels sits a thin layer of modern HTML chrome: frosted pills in Zen Maru Gothic that switch from light to dark with the sky, not with the operating system. Everything is lowercase. The garden is quiet by default and moves in small loops: drifting clouds, koi, falling leaves, fireflies, a flickering lantern, a ripple when a task finishes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One canvas at about 190 garden pixels tall (landscape) or 130 wide (portrait), integer-scaled with pixelated rendering.
- Palette swap, not filters: 35 scene roles, each with day, dusk and night values, interpolated by the hour.
- Ordered 4x4 Bayer dithering for every soft transition: sky bands, sun halo, pond depth, lantern glow, contact shadows.
- Outlined pets that stay brighter than any scene at any hour.
- Frosted, lowercase, rounded chrome that follows the sky.
- Deterministic layout: one stepping stone per pet, earlier members never move.

## Colors

A cozy 16-bit palette: muted, warm scenery that relights by the clock, with saturated pets that never relight.

### Primary
- **Pet tones** (`sakura-*`, `matcha-*`, `rice-*`, `yuzu-*`, `sora-*`, `fuji-*`, `kinako-*`): one ramp per member colour, each as base, highlight and shade. A pet wears its member's colour; each species defaults to its own (sakura mochi, matcha tofu, rice onigiri, sora dango, yuzu taiyaki, kinako dorayaki), and an unknown colour falls back to that default. These are the colour of the product; they are the only saturated, high-value masses on screen and they ignore the clock.
- **Pet Ink** (`pet-ink`, dark plum): the 1 px outline around every pet silhouette, arms included, and the feet, eyes and mouths. Also outlines the sleeping z so it reads over water, gravel or sky.

### Secondary
- **Sprite accents**: white eye glints and `pet-blush` cheeks on every face; `laptop-lid` and `laptop-edge` for the work pose; `alert-red` on the white speech bubble of the waiting pose; `crumb-gold` and `crumb-blue` for eating; `sparkle` gold stars for cheering; `nori` for the onigiri band.
- **Lantern Gold** (`lantern-light`, with `lantern-dim` toward the edge): the toro's window and its small two-tone dithered pool of light at night. The same gold family returns in the chrome as `alert-ring`, so "light" and "needs you" share one warm signal.

### Tertiary
- **Ambient life**: `koi-orange` and `koi-white` with `koi-spot`; `heart` for boops; `firefly` at night; `star` and `star-dim` for the twinkling night sky. Each is a 1 to 5 pixel mark, never a mass.

### Neutral
- **Scene roles** (`<role>-day`, `<role>-dusk`, `<role>-night`): sky bands `sky0` to `sky3` (top to horizon), `far` and `snow` (the mountain), `forest`/`forestHi`, wall `tile`/`tileHi`/`plaster`/`plasterLine`, ground `gravel`/`rakeD`/`rakeL`, rock `stone`/`stoneD`/`stoneH`, `moss`/`mossD`/`mossH`, `bush`/`bushH`/`bloom`, maple `maple`/`mapleH`/`mapleD`/`trunk`, pond `water`/`waterD`/`waterH`/`pad`, `cloud`/`cloudD`, and `sun` (which is the moon at night). Most roles come as a base, a highlight (`H`/`Hi`) and a dark (`D`).
- **Day/dusk/night keyframes**: night until 05:00, night to dusk by 06:12, dusk to day by 07:30, day until 17:00, day to dusk by 18:36, dusk to night by 20:00. The scene counts as night when the night weight passes one half; that flag also switches stars, lantern light, fireflies and the chrome.
- **Chrome Ink and Paper** (`chrome-ink`, `chrome-paper`, `chrome-night-ink`): warm sumi black and washi white for all HTML text and solid pills, exposed to CSS as `--ink` and `--paper`. Plain pages (the landing page and privacy) are always light: ink on paper, whatever the OS theme. The page background behind the canvas is the current `gravel` colour, so overscroll and loading never flash a foreign colour.
- **Focus and selection**: the focus ring is `focus-ring` deep blue (4.6:1 or better on day and dusk gravel) and switches to `focus-ring-night` paper on night chips and dark plain pages; the garden pins its own ring so an OS dark theme never leaks into a daytime garden. Selected text sits on `selection`, the sakura pet's rim light, in chrome ink.

### Named Rules
**The Brighter Pet Rule.** Pets keep fixed tones and a 1 px `pet-ink` outline at every hour; the scene darkens around them, they never darken with it. A new pet colour gets a base, highlight and shade that out-value the day gravel's neighbourhood and still read against the night: base at 4.5:1 or better against night gravel, shade at 4.5:1 or better against `pet-ink` (enforced in `apps/web/test/pixel.test.ts`).

**The Palette Swap Rule.** Every scene colour is a role with exactly three values: day, dusk, night. A new scene element adds a role with all three or reuses one; it never darkens with a CSS filter, an overlay or alpha.

**The Ordered Dither Rule.** Soft light in the scene is made of whole pixels on a 4x4 Bayer pattern: sky bands, the sun's halo, pond depth, the lantern glow (full-colour gold pixels, not translucent ones) and the contact shadow under each pet. No smooth gradients, no alpha blending inside the canvas.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Zen Maru Gothic (with ui-rounded, system-ui)
**Body Font:** the system UI face (SF Pro on Apple, Segoe UI, Roboto), for body copy and every pill
**Hand Font:** Gaegu 700, for margin notes on the landing page only
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace), for commands only

**Character:** a soft, rounded Japanese gothic for headings, names and the wordmark, the system face for everything you read or press (its metrics centre cleanly in a pill), a hand for the odd note in the margin, and a mono that marks the one thing to type. The rounded display matches the pets' squishy silhouettes without imitating pixel type.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, 1.125rem): the `burrow` wordmark beside the mark, the notice titles, "get a pet". On the privacy page the same face carries the h1 (700, 2.25rem, tight tracking) and h2 (700, 1.5rem).
- **Tag** (700, clamp(12px, 1vw, 15px), line-height 1): the pet's name; its verb follows at 500 and 75% opacity. Detail text on hover or focus drops to 500 at 0.85em.
- **Body** (400, 0.875rem): notice body copy at 80% opacity; the privacy page runs 1.125rem lead and default body at max-w-2xl.
- **Label** (500, 0.75rem): nav link, status pill, the one-time hint.
- **Command** (Geist Mono, 13px in the pill, 0.875rem elsewhere): `npx burrow-pets init` and other CLI strings, always copyable where they appear in the garden.

### Named Rules
**The Lowercase Rule.** Interface copy is lowercase, warm and short: names, verbs ("hanging out", "needs you", "asleep"), status ("live", "reconnecting…"), titles ("nobody lives here yet."). Proper product nouns in prose (Claude Code) keep their case.

**The Mono Means Type-This Rule.** Geist Mono appears only on strings the visitor would type or paste. It never sets names, labels or headings.

## Layout

**The Integer Scale Rule.** The scene is drawn at logical resolution and scaled by a whole number S = max(2, round(viewport height / 190)) on landscape, or max(2, round(viewport width / 130)) on portrait. Width rounds up (overflow is clipped), height rounds down so the page never scrolls by a pixel. Never scale by a fraction and never smooth: `image-rendering: pixelated` on every canvas, including the 32 px header mark.

Spatial model (all in garden pixels, from `layoutGarden`):
- **Bands.** Horizon at min(92, 0.47H) landscape or min(96, 0.27H) portrait; the sky and mountain above, a wall from horizon to horizon+17, raked gravel from `gravelTop` (horizon + 17) down.
- **Fixed landmarks.** Pond centred at 53% width (landscape, rx ≤ 48, ry 11) or 50% (portrait, rx ≤ 46, ry 12); a lone pet or pair lowers the portrait pond to 45% of the gravel, larger burrows lift it to 32%. Lantern at 93% / 86% width, maple at 78% / 80%, Fuji at 30% / 32%, sun or moon at 18% / 20% width, a third of the way down the sky.
- **Focal spot.** The first member always takes the front-and-centre spot: 50% width, 22 px below the pond's front edge (or the front row limit). Then handpicked spots flank the pond, then regular rows.
- **Keep-outs.** A spot is valid only if it is at least 12 px below `gravelTop`, inside the side margin max(16, tagGap/2), outside the pond grown by 12 px across and 8 px deep, and clear of every placed pet (36 px row pitch vertically, or tagGap horizontally). The last on-screen row stays above `bottomReserve` = ceil((130 portrait / 70 landscape) / S) + 12, so tags clear the fixed command pill and the phone hint.
- **Tag gap.** tagGap = max(26, ceil(150 / S)) garden pixels, so 150 CSS px name tags never overlap in a row. Odd rows are offset by half a spacing.
- **Growth.** Up to 24 members. Rows past the first screen start below the fold and extend the gravel; the page scrolls instead of shrinking pets. Earlier members keep their stones as others join.
- **Props yield to pets.** Bushes skip any x within 22 px of a pet in the first 40 px of gravel; moss islands keep clear of heads and tags; raked rings circle every stone, the pond and the lantern.

Chrome sits on fixed insets: header 12 px (20 px from `sm`), the bottom pill 16 px from the bottom (24 px from `sm`; centred on phones, left-aligned from `sm`), hint above the pill on phones and bottom-right from `sm`. Embedded in the landing page's frame the garden has no chrome, no hint and no bottom reserve. Tags hang 6 garden pixels below each pet's feet, centred on the stone.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth inside the canvas is painted, not cast: layer order (sky, clouds, ground, pond life, lantern glow, pets back to front by their y, leaves, fireflies, hearts), upper-left rim light on every lit mass, a darker `D` role on undersides, and a dithered contact shadow under each pet mixed from `stoneD` toward deep plum. The chrome is the only thing that floats: frosted glass with one soft ambient shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Chip float** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(38, 36, 31, 0.12)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(1.3)`): every header pill, tag, command pill, hint and notice.
- **Alert ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(240, 198, 78, 0.8), 0 6px 20px rgba(20, 18, 30, 0.25)`): the waiting pet's tag only.

### Named Rules
**The Glass Is Chrome Rule.** Blur, translucency and box shadows exist only on HTML chrome over the canvas. The scene itself is opaque pixels. With reduced transparency the chips fall back to solid `#faf9f5` by day and `#181a20` by night.

**The Sky-Follows-Chrome Rule.** Chips switch to their night skin when the scene is night, regardless of the OS theme.

## Shapes

Two shape languages that never mix. In the canvas everything is built from whole pixels: ellipses and blobs rasterised with hash noise on their edges, stepped silhouettes, 1 px lines; no anti-aliasing. In the chrome everything is a full pill (999px): header, nav, status, tags, command pill, copy button, hint. The only rectangle is a notice or code panel at 16px corners. The focus ring rounds to 8px.

## Components

### Pet sprite (signature)
Cozy, squishy, readable from across a desk.
- **Grid:** 32 x 32, horizontal centre column 16, feet baseline row 28 with 4 one-pixel feet at row 29 when grounded. Drawn at the stone so feet sit 1 px above it.
- **Silhouettes:** mochi a half-ellipse dome (rows 14 to 28); tofu a rounded rectangle (x 6 to 26, rows 14 to 28, 3 px corners); onigiri a rice triangle (rows 11 to 28) with a low nori band (rows 26 to 28), leaving rice between band and face; dango three dumplings a pixel apart on a skewer that shows between them and at both ends, the larger middle one carrying the face; taiyaki a fish-shaped cake facing left with a fanned tail, a small top fin and a dithered row of scales; dorayaki two pancakes with a solid dark anko band low between them.
- **Light:** shade on the bottom two rows and the right edge, highlight on the top and left edges, a 2 px glint high on the left. Shading reads a snapshot of the silhouette, so it never cascades.
- **Face:** at row 21 (mochi) or 20 (tofu, onigiri). Open eyes are 2 x 2 with a white top-left glint; closed eyes are a 2 px line; happy eyes are a 3 px caret. Blush two pixels either side, three rows down. Mouth changes with state.
- **Outline:** every empty pixel 4-connected to the silhouette (arms included) becomes `pet-ink`; props are drawn over it.
- **States, frames and rate** (frames @ fps; pets offset by 1.7 frames each so a row never moves in unison):
  - idle 4 @ 3: breathe squash on frame 1, blink on frame 3, small smile.
  - eat 4 @ 5: crumb sparkle, open mouth chew, big squash, happy eyes.
  - wave 2 @ 4: right arm out and up, white speech bubble with a red "!" above the head, open mouth.
  - work 2 @ 6: low laptop lid (4 rows) in front, paws tapping alternately, breathes on frame 1. **Quiet** variant (no events for 2 min): same pose, eyes closed, paws still, outlined z.
  - sleep 3 @ 1.5: closed eyes, breathing squash, an outlined z climbing.
  - cheer 4 @ 8: squash then a 3 to 5 px jump with both arms up, open smile with tongue, two gold sparkles.
- **Reduced motion:** frame 0 of each state, which still carries the state's prop and face.

### Growth forms
Every species comes in three forms; the catalog shows all 18.
- **Baby** (under 100 xp): the grown silhouette at 78% about the feet, arms and feet scaled with it, the face on a per-species row (dango with closer eyes) that drops a row when the body squashes, so eyes never leave the body. Every state's props still fit.
- **Grown** (100 xp): the forms above.
- **Evolved** (400 xp): the grown form plus an outlined signature detail resting on the head's actual top, so it rides along with squashes and jumps: a pale sakura blossom (mochi), a green onion sprig (tofu), an umeboshi plum with a leaf (onigiri), a dollop of mitarashi glaze (dango), a golden crown fin (taiyaki), a chestnut (dorayaki). Two still gold sparkles sit beside it except while cheering.
- **Silhouette:** an undiscovered form is every drawn pixel in one flat colour (ink at 18% by day, paper at 22% at night) under a "?".
- Pets made before growth existed are grown, so nobody's pet shrinks.

### Name tag
The glance signal and a control.
- **Shape:** full pill, display face, `tag-pad` padding, centred under the stone.
- **Content:** name in 700, verb in 500 at 75% opacity; on hover or focus it grows a second line of detail ("waiting on you for 3m", "quiet for 4m, maybe interrupted").
- **Skins:** day chip, night chip, and the alert skin for the waiting state: dark ink fill, paper text, gold verb and a 3 px gold ring. Only one state ever gets the alert skin.
- **Hover / Focus:** lifts 2 px and scales to 1.04 over 0.2s on `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, using the `translate`/`scale` properties so it composes with the centring translate; a dotted white ring appears on the pet's stone. No transition with reduced motion.

### Garden interactions
- **Boop:** click a pet or its tag. A 0.45s hop of up to 5 px and three 5 x 5 pixel hearts rise and blink out.
- **Pond tap:** a ripple at the tap point and the koi swim three times faster for 0.9s.
- **Task finished:** a ripple crosses the pond on the pet's side when a stop event lands.

### Ambient motion
All loops run in one `requestAnimationFrame`, pause when the tab is hidden, and clamp each step at 50 ms. Every motion is in garden pixels per second, stepped by the real frame time (`apps/web/lib/pixel/ambient.ts`), so 120 Hz screens move at the same speed as 60 Hz ones. Ripples age by the clock, so one queued while the tab was hidden is dropped, not played minutes later.
- **Day:** 3 clouds (2 on portrait) drift right at 1.8 to 3.3 px/s and wrap.
- **Night:** about one star per three columns of width, twinkling white to `star-dim`, skipping the moon; up to 9 fireflies blinking on slow sine paths; the lantern halo flickers between two baked densities.
- **Always:** 2 koi circle the pond with a flicking tail; 14 two-pixel glints re-scatter on the water every 0.7s; up to 10 maple leaves fall at 9 px/s with a sway, rest on the gravel for 9s, then vanish.
- **Reduced motion:** clouds, koi and pet frames freeze; stars hold white; the lantern holds its bright density; no leaves, no fireflies.

### Performance (60 fps with 24 pets)
- Sky, ground (mountain, wall, maple, gravel, bushes, pond, stones, contact shadows, lantern), the lantern alone and the two glow densities are baked into offscreen canvases once per layout and palette step (the hour rounded to 0.1). Each frame is a few `drawImage` calls plus the small ambient marks.
- Sprites are cached as one 32 px canvas per species, colour, state, frame and quiet flag.
- The garden canvas carries `will-change: transform` so the frosted chrome above it does not re-blur it every frame.
- Resizes settle for 120 ms, unchanged sizes are ignored, and on touch screens a height-only change under 15% (the URL bar) never re-lays out the garden.

### Accessibility
- The canvas is `aria-hidden`; tags are the interactive and announced layer, and a visually hidden list restates every pet's state in text. The tab title names whoever is waiting.
- Focus ring: 2 px `focus-ring` blue, 3 px offset, 8px corners, on every focusable element.
- Reduced motion and reduced transparency are honoured as above. State never relies on colour or motion alone: every state has a distinct pose, a prop and a word.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** draw new scene elements into the baked layers with palette roles that have day, dusk and night values.
- **Do** keep every pet at a whole-pixel position, inside a 32 px grid, outlined 1 px in `pet-ink`, on its own stepping stone with a dithered contact shadow.
- **Do** give each new state a distinct pose and a prop that survives frame 0, plus a lowercase verb for its tag.
- **Do** place new landmarks and props outside pet keep-outs, the way bushes and moss already yield to stones and tags.
- **Do** make soft light with ordered dithering and full-colour pixels.
- **Do** switch chrome with the scene's night flag, and keep chrome as frosted pills with names and titles in Zen Maru Gothic.
- **Do** keep ambient motion as small loops gated by reduced motion and paused when hidden.

### Don't:
- **Don't** scale the scene by a fraction or let the browser smooth it.
- **Don't** darken, tint or blur the canvas with CSS filters, overlays or alpha; swap the palette instead.
- **Don't** relight the pets with the clock or let a scene colour out-shine them.
- **Don't** use `pet-ink` outlines on scenery; scenery edges use their own `D` roles.
- **Don't** put glass, shadows or rounded pills inside the canvas, or pixel art inside the chrome beyond the header mark.
- **Don't** give any state other than "needs you" the gold-ringed alert tag.
- **Don't** shrink pets or overlap tags to fit more members; grow the garden downward.
- **Don't** add underground, soil or dirt scenes.
